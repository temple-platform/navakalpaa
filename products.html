Our first product

Setu

In development

Simplifying temple operations and devotee services.

Setu is temple management software in development from Navakalpaa Tech LLP. It is being built so a devotee can book a seva from a temple QR code, in Telugu or English, and so counter staff, poojaris and administrators can run that work.
