About Navakalpaa Tech LLP

Technology for the problems people have accepted as complex.

Navakalpaa Tech LLP is an independent technology company. We look for practical problems and build useful products and software services to address them.

Our purpose

To simplify everyday problems through well-designed technology products.

What we do

Navakalpaa Tech LLP builds, launches and supports technology products, SaaS applications and software services.

Each product can have its own name, look and audience while remaining part of the Navakalpaa portfolio. Our first product is Setu, temple management software for temple operations and devotee services.

Our approach

Three principles guide how we build.

  1. 01

    Understand the problem

    Start with the real-world problem, not the technology.

  2. 02

    Design with purpose

    Make solutions intuitive, practical, and easy to use.

  3. 03

    Build for real use

    Focus on usefulness, reliability, and continuous improvement.
